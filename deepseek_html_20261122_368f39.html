The Story

One night. One old game.
Two brothers who never came back.

You and your brother were playing an old forgotten game when the screen began to burn. You fell through light and static — into the game itself.

Emberkin is a world of crypts and ember woods where the last flame is guttering out. Only heroes from beyond can relight it. So you took up the blade, and began your journey.

When the Lich falls, his dying breath curses you and drags your brother into the abyss. Five floors below, the Void King holds him in shadow. You lost him once. You will not lose him again.

Features

Built like a real action-RPG

No engine, no build step, no dependencies beyond a Google font and a Tailwind CDN. Just canvas and care.

Procedural floors

Each floor is generated as a room graph — walls, doors, pillar layouts and treasure all randomised on entry.

3-hit combo system

Chain three slashes within 0.65s for a heavy finisher with extra range, double damage, and a bigger knockback.

Perfect dodge & weak points

Dash through an attack at the last moment for a "PERFECT!" bonus. Staggered enemies take 2× damage for a few seconds.

Achievements & shards

Earn 30+ hidden achievements — first blood, 10-hit combos, perfect dodges, wave 20 — and collect shards to buy permanent upgrades.

Relic-unlocked spells

Find three relics hidden across the chapters to unlock three extra spells — Meteor, Inferno and Time Stop.

Mobile-ready

Virtual joystick, on-screen slash/dash buttons, and three spell buttons with radial cooldowns. Auto-detects touch.

Spellbook

Three slots. Nine options.

Six spells are available from the start. Three more unlock as you find relics — pick any three before each run.

Fireball 20 MP · 1.5s

Hurl a blazing orb that explodes on impact. Area damage, chains through enemies.

Frost Nova 30 MP · 6s

A ring of ice hits everything near you and freezes them in place.

Thunder 35 MP · 5s

Lightning chains through up to four foes, each bolt jumping to the next.

Heal 40 MP · 12s

Restore two hearts with a burst of light. Best saved for a panic moment.

Blink 15 MP · 3s

Teleport forward and dodge all damage for half a second. Great gap-closer.

Ward 30 MP · 10s

A shield blocks the next three hits over six seconds. Pure defensive layer.

RELIC
Meteor 50 MP · 8s

Call meteors down on up to five foes. Huge area damage on impact.

A wall of flame scorches everything in front of you and burns them.

RELIC

Freeze all foes and wipe their projectiles. A get-out-of-jail card.

Relic spells only appear in the picker once you've found the corresponding relic.

Tips for a better run

Advice
  • • Kill enemies in quick succession to build a score combo. The multiplier stacks up to 5×.
  • • Dash through an attack at the last moment for a PERFECT dodge — it slows the room for a beat and rewards bonus points.
  • • Watch for the WEAK! marker over staggered enemies — you deal double damage while it's up.
  • • Bosses have two phases. When their health drops below half, expect a new attack pattern.
  • • Save at least one defensive spell — Ward or Blink — for boss rooms.
  • • Grab shards dropped by enemies and bosses to buy permanent upgrades between runs.

FAQ

Questions from the fire

Is this a real game or a demo?

It's a real, complete game — five chapters with multiple floors each, eight named bosses, a proper ending, endless mode, achievements, permanent upgrades, relics, spells, and saves. It just happens to also fit in one HTML file, so you can double-click it and play.

How do I unlock the three extra spells?

Three relics are hidden in cleared rooms across the chapters — they have a small chance to drop after any normal room is fully cleared. Grab one and the corresponding spell (Meteor, Inferno or Time Stop) becomes selectable in the spell picker. Find all three to have all nine spells available.

What are shards for?

Enemies and bosses drop shards. Spend them in the Upgrades menu on permanent boosts — more starting HP, faster dash cooldown, and extra melee damage. Upgrades persist across every run and difficulty.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The game uses viewport-fit=cover, a virtual joystick, and on-screen buttons with safe-area insets for notched phones. You can toggle "tablet mode" in the menu if you want the virtual buttons on a PC or the keyboard hints on a tablet.

Where is progress saved?

In localStorage on the browser you play in: best score, best endless wave, unlocked chapters, shards, upgrades, unlocked relics, achievements, chosen abilities, and tablet/PC preference. Clearing site data resets everything.

Can I edit it / learn from it?

Yes. Everything is plain JavaScript at the top of the file: the sprite table, the enemy table, the chapter list, the boss HP, the spell array. Tweak any of those and the game updates. The engine is a straightforward update / draw loop on a 256×208 canvas.
